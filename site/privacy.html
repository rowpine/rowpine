Privacy

What this website and the Rowpine package collect, and what they do not.

The rowpine package

The rowpine npm package and the components from the shadcn registry make no network calls. Excel, PDF and CSV files are built in your users' browsers, and the license key is checked there too, against a public key in the package. Your table data never reaches us.

This website

rowpine.com counts visits with Umami, an analytics service that sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. It records the page, the referring site, and the browser, operating system, device type and country, and clicks on a few buttons such as the checkout buttons. It does not record your IP address or anything that identifies you.

If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, the analytics script is not loaded at all.

Pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and fonts come from Google Fonts; both receive the usual request data, such as your IP address, when your browser loads a page.

Buying a license

Checkout runs on PayPal, under PayPal's own privacy policy. We receive the name, email address and note you give PayPal, and use them to send the license key, answer support requests and keep the records the law requires. We do not sell or share them.
