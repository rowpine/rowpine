FAQ

Questions

Who needs a license?

Each developer who writes code that uses Rowpine's Pro features. The people who use your app do not, and there is no limit on apps, users or servers.

What happens after the year of updates?

Everything keeps working. Your key covers every version released during your year, for good. A version released after it runs too, with the notice line, until you renew updates.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes. Install it and use everything. Without a key, Excel and PDF files carry one notice line and the console shows a message once. Nothing is switched off.

How is the key checked?

In the browser, against a public key in the package. Rowpine never calls a server, so it works offline and behind firewalls.

How do I pay, and when do I get the key?

Checkout is on PayPal: pay with a PayPal account, or with a card without one where PayPal offers it. Put the developer's email in the note if it differs from the payer's. The license key is sent by email to that address.

Do I get the source?

The export menu, pivot table and pivot designer install from the shadcn registry as source in your project. The engine (file writers and pivot) is the rowpine npm package.

Which versions of TanStack Table and shadcn/ui?

TanStack Table v8 and v9, and shadcn/ui on Base UI or Radix. The test suite runs on each.
